Glogg

Raw Recipe (In Development)

Traditional Scandinavian Glögg (Classic Version) Ingredients 1 bottle dry red wine (750 ml) 1 cup aquavit or vodka (0.5 cup to milder) 0.5 cup granulated sugar (adjust to taste) 2 cinnamon sticks 6–8 whole cloves 1 tbsp cardamom pods, lightly crushed 1 small piece fresh ginger (optional but lovely) 1 strip orange peel (no white pith) Optional: 1 star anise (not strictly traditional, but festive ✨) For serving Blanched almonds, chopped Golden raisins Method Infuse the spirits Combine aquavit/vodka, sugar, and all spices in a jar or bowl. Cover and let steep overnight (or up to 48 hours) at room temp. 👉 This is where the magic deepens. Strain Remove spices and orange peel. Heat gently Add the spiced alcohol to the red wine in a pot. Warm very gently over low heat — never boil (we want cozy, not cooked). Taste & adjust More sugar? A splash more spirits? Trust your festive instincts 🎄 Serve Ladle into mugs, add a spoonful of raisins and chopped almonds. 🌟 Pro Nordic Tips Traditionally, glögg is stronger and sweeter than other mulled wines. It gets better after a day or two, so it’s perfect for prepping ahead. Serve with ginger snaps (pepparkakor) or saffron buns for full Scandinavian vibes.
