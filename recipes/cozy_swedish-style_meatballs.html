Cozy Swedish Style Meatballs

Raw Recipe (In Development)

Cozy Swedish-Style Meatballs (Julköttbullar) Ingredients 250 g ground beef 250 g ground pork ½ cup milk ½ cup breadcrumbs 1 small onion, very finely chopped 1 egg 1 tsp salt ½ tsp white or black pepper ¼ tsp allspice (very Christmassy ✨) Butter for frying Method Soak breadcrumbs in milk for 5 minutes. Add onion, egg, spices, and meat. Mix gently — don’t overwork. Roll small balls (they should be bite-sized). Fry slowly in butter until golden and cooked through. Quick Gravy (optional but recommended 😌) Use the pan drippings Add 1 tbsp flour → whisk Add ~1 cup beef stock + a splash of cream Simmer until silky Serve with: Boiled potatoes Lingonberry jam (or cranberry sauce in a pinch)

Braised Red Cabbage with Apple
Ingredients
½ small red cabbage, sliced
1 apple, sliced
25 g butter
2 tbsp red wine vinegar
1 tbsp lingonberry jam
1 tsp sugar
2 whole cloves
Pinch cinnamon
Salt
Method
Melt butter, add the cabbage & apple.
Add remaining ingredients.
Cover and simmer 45–60 min, stirring occasionally.
