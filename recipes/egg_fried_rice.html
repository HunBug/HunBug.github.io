🍳 Egg Fried Rice with Mushrooms & Peas

Serves: 2–3 | Time: ~25 minutes

🛒 Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice (cold and crumbly, ideally a day old)
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 100–150 g white mushrooms (like your pack), sliced ~½ cm thick
  • 1/2 cup frozen peas, thawed
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 onion or 2 scallions, chopped
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil (e.g. canola, sunflower)
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame oil (for finishing)
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • (Optional garnish): extra scallions, chili oil, lime wedge, sesame seeds

🧼 Prep Checklist (Before Cooking)

✅ Thaw the peas (rinse in warm water and pat dry) ✅ Wipe mushrooms clean with damp paper towel and slice ✅ Beat the eggs in a bowl ✅ Break up the cold rice with hands or fork to loosen clumps

👨‍🍳 Cooking Instructions

1. Soft-Scramble the Eggs

  • Heat ½ tbsp oil in a large nonstick pan over medium-high heat.
  • Add the beaten eggs.
  • Stir gently until they’re about 70% cooked (soft and glossy, a little runny).
  • Remove to a plate and set aside.

2. Cook the Aromatics

  • Add 1 tbsp oil to the same pan.
  • Add garlic and onions/scallions.
  • Sauté for 30–60 seconds until fragrant and soft.

3. Fry the Mushrooms

  • Add a small splash of oil if the pan looks dry.
  • Add sliced mushrooms in a single layer.
  • Let them sit undisturbed for 1.5–2 minutes until golden.
  • Flip or stir, and cook the other side for 1–2 minutes.
  • Add a small pinch of salt during this step.

4. Add Peas

  • Add thawed peas to the pan.
  • Stir and cook for 1 minute until hot and bright green.

5. Add the Rice

  • Crumble in your cold rice.
  • Break up clumps with your spatula.
  • Spread the rice evenly and let it sit for 30–45 seconds to get a bit crispy.
  • Stir and repeat 2–3 times, letting the rice toast slightly between each mix.

6. Season the Rice

  • Add 2 tbsp soy sauce and a pinch of pepper.
  • Stir well to evenly coat the rice.
  • Taste and adjust (add a splash more soy sauce or a touch of salt if needed).

7. Reunite the Eggs

  • Add the soft scrambled eggs back in.
  • Stir gently for 30–60 seconds to finish cooking and mix through the rice.

8. Finish & Serve

  • Drizzle with toasted sesame oil (1 tsp).
  • Optional: Garnish with scallions, chili oil, lime wedge, or sesame seeds.
  • Serve hot and enjoy every flavorful bite! 🍚🍄🍳
