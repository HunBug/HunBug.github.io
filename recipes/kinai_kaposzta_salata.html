🥬 Cold & Crunchy Chili Cabbage Salad — Adjusted for 300g Cabbage

🛒 Ingredients (Updated for 300g cabbage)

  • 300g cabbage, finely shredded
  • 1 medium carrot, grated (or about 70–100g)
  • 1 tsp salt (for sweating — downscaled to prevent over-salting)
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp white wine vinegar (or plain white vinegar)
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil (sunflower, rapeseed, etc.)
  • 3/4 tsp chili flakes (or adjust to taste)
  • A few grinds of black pepper
  • (Optional garnish) Extra chili flakes or chili oil on top

🔪 Preparation Steps

1. Sweat the Cabbage

  • In a large bowl, mix 300g shredded cabbage with 1 tsp salt.
  • Let it sit for 30 minutes, tossing once or twice.
  • This softens the cabbage and draws out water while keeping it crisp.

2. Drain & Squeeze

  • Gently squeeze the cabbage by hand (or in a sieve) to remove excess moisture.
  • Return to a clean bowl.

3. Add the Carrot

  • Mix in the grated carrot.

4. Make the Dressing

In a small pan, gently heat:

  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp vinegar
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 3/4 tsp chili flakes
  • A few grinds of black pepper

Once it begins to bubble, stir to dissolve the sugar, then immediately pour over the cabbage and carrot mix.

5. Toss & Marinate

  • Mix thoroughly to coat everything.
  • Let rest at room temp for 15–20 minutes, then chill for 30–60 minutes in the fridge.
