Cachelos Con Chorizo

Raw Recipe (In Development)

🐖✨ 1) Mustard & Herb Roast Pork Loin + Cachelos con Chorizo

⏱ Total Time

45–60 min

🛒 Ingredients (for 4 people)

For the pork roast

600–800 g pork loin (whole piece)

2 tbsp Dijon mustard

2 cloves garlic, minced

1 tbsp olive oil

1 tsp rosemary or thyme (fresh or dried)

1 tsp smoked paprika (optional)

Salt & pepper

100 ml white wine or broth

For the cachelos con chorizo

900–1,000 g potatoes, peeled & in chunks

200 g chorizo (mild or spicy)

1 tsp salt (for boiling water)

Olive oil

½–1 tsp paprika (for serving)

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Prepare the pork

  2. Preheat oven to 200°C / 390°F.

  3. Mix mustard, garlic, olive oil, herbs, paprika, salt & pepper.

  4. Rub the pork loin completely with the mixture.

  5. Sear

  6. Heat a skillet with a little oil.

  7. Sear the loin 2–3 min per side until lightly golden.

  8. Roast

  9. Transfer to baking tray or use oven-safe pan.

  10. Add 100 ml wine or broth to the bottom.

  11. Roast 20–30 min, until internal temp 65–70°C.

  12. Rest 10 min before slicing.

  13. Make the cachelos while the pork roasts

  14. Add potatoes + whole chorizo to a pot.

  15. Cover with water, add 1 tsp salt.

  16. Simmer gently 25–30 min until potatoes are soft.

  17. Drain. Slice the chorizo.

  18. Drizzle potatoes with olive oil & sprinkle paprika.

  19. Serve

Plate sliced pork, potatoes, and chorizo. Spoon a bit of pan juices over the pork.

🍲🔥 2) Carne ao Caldeiro–Style Stewed Pork Loin + Cachelos con Chorizo

⏱ Total Time

60 min (40 min simmering)

🛒 Ingredients (for 4 people)

For the pork stew

600–800 g pork loin, cut into 3–4 cm chunks

1 large onion, sliced

3 cloves garlic, minced

1 bay leaf

1–2 tsp smoked paprika

150 ml white wine

500 ml broth

2 tbsp olive oil

Salt & pepper

Optional: carrot slices

Optional: a handful or two of cabbage (recommended!)

For the cachelos con chorizo

900–1,000 g potatoes, peeled & chunked

200 g chorizo

1 tsp salt (for boiling)

Olive oil & paprika for serving

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Start the stew

  2. Heat olive oil in a pot.

  3. Add pork, season with salt & pepper, brown lightly (5–6 min).

  4. Add sliced onion, cook until soft.

  5. Add garlic + paprika (stir 10 seconds).

  6. Add wine, reduce by half.

  7. Add broth + bay leaf (+ carrots if using).

  8. Simmer gently 30–40 min.

  9. Add cabbage (optional but delicious)

Add the cabbage in the last 15–20 min of simmering.

  1. Make the cachelos while stew simmers

  2. In another pot: potatoes + whole chorizo + 1 tsp salt + water.

  3. Simmer 25–30 min until potatoes are tender.

  4. Drain. Slice chorizo.

  5. Drizzle potatoes with olive oil + sprinkle paprika.

  6. Finish the stew

Remove bay leaf.

If broth is thin, simmer uncovered 5 more minutes.

If thick, add a splash of wine or broth.

  1. Serve

On one plate:

Pork stew with broth

Potatoes

Chorizo slices

Extra paprika & olive oil to finish

It’s pure Galician comfort. 💛
