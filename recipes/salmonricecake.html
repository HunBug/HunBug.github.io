🐟 Salmon Rice Cakes with Garlic Yogurt Dip

(No leftovers, all from scratch)

Serves: 2–3 people (6–8 cakes) Prep Time: ~20 minutes Cook Time: ~20 minutes Total Time: ~40 minutes

🥣 Ingredients

For the Rice

  • 1 cup uncooked rice (short-grain, sushi, or regular white rice)
  • 2 cups water
  • ½ tsp salt

You’ll end up with about 2½–3 cups cooked rice; you’ll use around 1½ cups of it for the cakes. The rest can be saved/eaten on the side.

For the Salmon Cakes (Raw Salmon Version)

  • 200 g raw salmon fillet, skinless and boneless
  • 1½ cups cooked rice (still slightly warm or at least not rock-cold)
  • 1 large egg
  • 3–4 tbsp breadcrumbs (start with 3, add more if mixture is too soft)

  • 2 tbsp finely chopped fresh or frozen herbs

  • e.g. dill, parsley, chives, or a mix

  • ½ tsp Dijon mustard (optional, but recommended)
  • 2 tbsp finely grated hard cheese (Parmesan, Grana Padano, etc.) – optional but tasty
  • Salt and black pepper to taste (start with ½ tsp salt and ¼ tsp pepper)
  • 2–3 tbsp neutral oil for frying (sunflower, canola, etc.)

For the Garlic Yogurt Dip

  • 150 g Greek yogurt or plain natural yogurt
  • 1 small garlic clove, finely grated or crushed
  • 1–2 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp finely chopped dill, parsley, or chives
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Optional: a drizzle of olive oil on top

👩‍🍳 Instructions

1. Cook the Rice

  1. Rinse the rice in cold water until the water runs mostly clear.
  2. Add rice, water, and ½ tsp salt to a pot. Bring to a boil.
  3. Once boiling, reduce to low heat, cover with a lid, and cook for about 12–15 minutes until the water is absorbed.
  4. Turn off the heat and let it sit, covered, for another 5–10 minutes.
  5. Fluff with a fork and let it cool a bit. You want it warm or room temp, not piping hot.

2. Prepare the Salmon (Raw Version)

  1. Check the salmon for any remaining bones and remove them.

  2. Cut it into small cubes, then either:

  3. Finely chop with a knife until it’s almost like a coarse mince, or

  4. Pulse briefly in a food processor (don’t overdo it; you don’t want paste).
  5. Pat dry gently with a paper towel if it’s very wet.

3. Make the Salmon Rice Mixture

  1. In a large bowl, add:

  2. 1½ cups cooked rice

  3. Minced/chopped salmon
  4. 1 egg
  5. 3 tbsp breadcrumbs
  6. Herbs
  7. Dijon mustard (if using)
  8. Grated cheese (if using)
  9. Salt and pepper
  10. Mix well with a spoon or your hands until everything is evenly combined.

  11. Check the texture:

  12. If it feels too loose or wet, add another 1 tbsp breadcrumbs (or two) until it holds together when you press it.

  13. It should be soft but shapeable, like a slightly sticky meatball mixture.

4. Shape & Chill the Cakes

  1. With slightly damp hands, form 6–8 patties, about the size of your palm and around 1–1.5 cm thick.
  2. Place them on a plate or tray.
  3. If you have time, refrigerate for 10–15 minutes – this helps them set and makes frying easier.

5. Fry the Salmon Rice Cakes

  1. Heat 2–3 tbsp oil in a non-stick pan over medium heat.
  2. When the oil is hot, gently add the patties. Don’t overcrowd the pan.

  3. Fry for about 4–5 minutes per side, until:

  4. Deep golden brown on the outside

  5. Salmon cooked through in the middle (it will turn opaque and firm)
  6. Place them on a plate lined with paper towel to remove excess oil.

6. Make the Garlic Yogurt Dip

  1. In a small bowl, combine:

  2. Yogurt

  3. Garlic
  4. Lemon juice
  5. Herbs
  6. Salt & pepper

  7. Stir until smooth. Taste and adjust:

  8. More lemon for acidity

  9. More salt/pepper for punch
  10. Drizzle a bit of olive oil on top and sprinkle extra herbs if you like.

7. Serve

  • Serve the warm salmon rice cakes with the garlic yogurt dip on the side.

  • Lovely with:

  • A green salad

  • Steamed or roasted vegetables
  • Or just lots of toasted bread for dipping!

📝 Using Shop Sliced or Smoked Salmon Instead

If you want to use shop sliced salmon (like cold-smoked salmon slices or lightly cured):

  • Use 150–180 g smoked or sliced salmon, finely chopped.
  • Reduce the salt in the mixture (smoked salmon is already salty!). Start with just a little pinch, then taste a tiny bit of the raw mix.

  • Everything else stays the same, but:

  • The cakes will have a stronger, smokier flavor.

  • They’ll cook a bit faster since smoked salmon is already “cured” – you mostly need to bind and heat through, so 3–4 minutes per side may be enough.

You can also do a 50/50 mix: 100 g raw salmon + 80–100 g smoked, for a really nice depth of flavor.
