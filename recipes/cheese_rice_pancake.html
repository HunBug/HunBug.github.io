Cheese Rice Pancake

Raw Recipe (In Development)

Ah, the beautiful alchemy of leftovers! Turning humble rice and cheese into crispy, golden pancakes? Chef’s kiss. Let's revive and maybe even upgrade that happy accident with a tasty, crispy Cheesy Rice Pancake recipe — and I'll throw in a couple of dips to match their savory charm.

🧀 Crispy Cheesy Rice Pancakes Core Ingredients (base version):

2 cups cooked rice (any kind, day-old works best)

1 cup shredded cheese (cheddar, mozzarella, gouda, or a mix)

1 egg (for binding)

2–3 tbsp all-purpose flour or breadcrumbs (optional, for extra structure)

2 tbsp chopped green onion or chives (optional but delightful)

Salt & pepper to taste

Butter or oil for frying

Optional Add-ins for extra pizzazz:

A pinch of garlic powder or paprika

Chopped herbs (parsley, dill, cilantro)

Finely diced onion, bell pepper, or jalapeño for heat

Leftover cooked vegetables, finely chopped

🥄 Directions:

Mix It Up: In a bowl, combine rice, cheese, egg, flour (or breadcrumbs), green onions, and any spices or add-ins. Mix until it holds together. If it feels too loose, add a bit more flour or breadcrumbs.

Form Patties: Scoop about ¼ cup of the mixture and form into a small pancake or patty with your hands. Slightly flatten them — not too thin, or they'll break.

Pan-Fry to Perfection: Heat a skillet over medium heat and add a touch of oil or butter. Cook each patty for about 3–4 minutes per side until golden brown and crispy.

Drain & Serve: Place on a paper towel briefly to remove excess oil, then serve hot!

🥣 Matching Dips — pick your vibe: 1. Garlic Yogurt Herb Dip (cool & tangy)

½ cup plain Greek yogurt or sour cream

1 clove garlic, minced or grated

1 tsp lemon juice

Chopped fresh dill or parsley

Salt & pepper

Mix everything together and let sit for 10 min so flavors mingle. Perfect contrast to the warm, melty pancake.

  1. Spicy Sriracha Mayo (punchy & bold)

2 tbsp mayo

1 tsp sriracha (or more to taste)

½ tsp lemon juice or vinegar

Optional: pinch of sugar for balance

Stir it up. Great if your pancakes have a touch of heat or smoky cheese.

  1. Simple Tomato Relish (bright & fresh)

Chopped cherry tomatoes

A splash of balsamic or red wine vinegar

Olive oil

Salt, pepper, and basil or oregano

A quick-fix version of a chutney-style dip — zingy and vibrant.
