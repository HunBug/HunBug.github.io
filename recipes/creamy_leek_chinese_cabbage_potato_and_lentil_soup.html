Creamy Leek Chinese Cabbage Potato And Lentil Soup

Raw Recipe (In Development)

🥣 Creamy Leek, Chinese Cabbage, Potato & Lentil Soup

(Half-batch — 3 to 4 servings)

🧅 Ingredients (HALF of your recipe)

1 tablespoon olive oil (or ½ tbsp olive oil + ½ tbsp butter)

2 leeks, sliced (white + light green parts)

1 medium onion or shallot, chopped

1 celery stalk, chopped

2 garlic cloves, minced

1 medium zucchini, chopped

1–2 medium potatoes, peeled & cubed

1½–2 cups sliced Chinese cabbage (Napa cabbage)

½ cup cooked lentils (or ¼ cup dry lentils to cook)

1 teaspoon dried thyme

½ teaspoon ground cumin

Salt & pepper to taste

3½–4 cups vegetable broth

¼–½ cup oat milk OR cream

Juice of ½ lemon

Optional toppings: parsley, chives, drizzle of olive oil, swirl of cream

🍳 Instructions (same, just smaller pot!)

1️⃣ Sauté aromatics

Heat oil (and butter if using). Add:

leeks

onion

celery

garlic Cook 8–10 minutes until soft and fragrant.

2️⃣ Add vegetables

Add:

zucchini

potatoes

Chinese cabbage

Stir and cook 2–3 minutes.

3️⃣ Add seasoning & lentils

Add thyme, cumin, pepper, and the cooked lentils. Stir gently.

4️⃣ Add broth

Pour in 3½–4 cups broth. Simmer 20–30 minutes until potatoes are tender.

5️⃣ Blend

Blend half the soup for a creamy/chunky texture OR blend all for a silky smooth bowl.

6️⃣ Finish

Stir in:

oat milk or cream

lemon juice Taste and adjust seasoning.

7️⃣ Serve

Top with:

parsley or chives

olive oil or cream

crusty bread on the side

💛 What this half-batch tastes like

Sweet, mellow leeks

Creamy potatoes & lentils

Soft, silky Chinese cabbage

Warm herbal notes from thyme & cumin

Bright lemon finish

Still the same delicious cozy bowl, just the perfect amount.
