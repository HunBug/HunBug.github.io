🍌 Banana Nutella Rice Pudding (Korean-Inspired Style) Creamy, dreamy, with a gooey Nutella swirl and caramelized banana topping. Think of it like a Korean juk meets European decadence. 🍚 Ingredients: 1/2 cup short-grain rice (like sushi or sticky rice)

2 cups milk (or oat/almond milk)

1/2 cup water

2 tbsp sugar (or to taste)

Pinch of salt

1/2 tsp vanilla extract

2 tbsp Nutella (more for drizzling)

1 ripe banana (sliced)

Butter or oil for caramelizing banana

🔥 Instructions: Simmer the rice with milk, water, sugar, and salt on low heat, stirring often to prevent sticking. Cook until thick and creamy, ~25–30 minutes.

Add vanilla and Nutella: Stir in both once the rice is soft.

Caramelize the banana: Sauté banana slices in a bit of butter/oil until golden.

Assemble: Spoon pudding into bowls, top with caramelized banana, and drizzle with extra Nutella. Serve warm or chilled.

🥥🍗 Coconut Peanut Chicken Curry with Zucchini & Bell Pepper 🍽️ Serves 2–3 (or 1 with leftovers you’ll dream about)

🧂 Ingredients Leftover cooked chicken (shredded/chopped)

1 zucchini, sliced

1 bell pepper, sliced

1/2 onion (if you have it)

2 garlic cloves, minced

1 tsp grated ginger

1 tbsp peanut butter (smooth or crunchy)

1 can coconut milk

1 tbsp fish sauce (adjust to taste)

1 tbsp soy sauce

1 tsp sugar or honey

1 tbsp chili paste (adjust to taste)

1–2 tsp lime juice or mirin

Oil for cooking

🍳 How to Make It: Sauté aromatics In a large pan or wok, heat 1 tbsp oil. Add garlic, ginger, and onion (if using). Cook 1–2 minutes until fragrant.

Cook veggies Add zucchini and bell pepper. Stir-fry for 3–5 minutes until they start to soften.

Warm the chicken Add your leftover chicken to the pan, just enough to warm through.

Make the saucy magic Pour in the coconut milk, then stir in:

Peanut butter (let it melt in)

Soy sauce

Fish sauce

Chili paste

Sugar or honey

Simmer & season Let it simmer for 5–8 minutes, stirring occasionally. Taste and adjust: more chili for spice, fish sauce for saltiness, lime/mirin for acidity.

Finish with flair Drizzle a bit of sesame oil or fresh lime juice over the top before serving. Optional garnish: green onions, sesame seeds, crushed peanuts, or fresh herbs (if you’ve got them hiding somewhere).

🥣 Serve with: Steamed rice

Warmed couscous for a fusion vibe

Or even a crispy flatbread to scoop it all up

Ohhh, fantastic choice! 🥢✨ Homemade Okonomiyaki sauce is a game-changer — sweet, tangy, savory, and a breeze to whip up with pantry staples. Think of it as a Japanese-style sweet Worcestershire BBQ sauce — perfect not just for okonomiyaki but also drizzled on takoyaki, yaki onigiri, or even fries! Here’s how to make it from scratch:

🍶 Homemade Okonomiyaki Sauce 🧂 Ingredients: 3 tbsp ketchup

1½ tbsp Worcestershire sauce

1½ tbsp oyster sauce (or tonkatsu sauce if you have it)

1 tbsp soy sauce

1–2 tsp honey or sugar (adjust to taste)

Optional: ½ tsp mirin or rice vinegar (for extra depth)

🥄 Instructions: Mix it up: In a small bowl, combine ketchup, Worcestershire sauce, oyster sauce, soy sauce, and honey (or sugar). Stir until smooth and glossy.

Taste & adjust: Want it sweeter? Add a touch more honey. Want it tangier? A splash of rice vinegar or mirin does wonders.

Ready to use: Drizzle generously over your hot, fluffy okonomiyaki, top with mayo, sprinkle with bonito flakes and aonori — and you’re golden!

💡 Chef’s Note: If you don’t have oyster sauce, you can increase Worcestershire and soy sauce slightly and add a pinch of dashi powder or anchovy paste for umami.

Store leftover sauce in a small jar in the fridge — it keeps well for a week or so.

Ohhh, excellent choice! 🌟✨ Let’s turn those humble lentils into a rich, cozy lentil curry — deeply spiced, creamy, and perfect with rice or naan. This is the type of dish that makes you feel hugged from the inside. 🥰🍛

🍛 Comforting Lentil Curry (Easy One-Pot) 🧂 Ingredients: 1 cup red lentils (or green/brown; red cook faster & get creamy)

1 medium onion, finely chopped

2–3 cloves garlic, minced

1 inch fresh ginger, grated

1–2 medium tomatoes, diced (or 1/2 can diced tomatoes)

1 small zucchini or bell pepper, diced (optional, but perfect with it!)

1–2 tbsp curry powder (or a mix: 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp coriander, 1/2 tsp paprika)

1/2 tsp chili flakes or fresh chili (optional, for heat)

1 cup coconut milk (or plain yogurt or cream)

2–3 cups vegetable broth or water

2 tbsp oil or butter

1–2 tbsp soy sauce (or salt to taste)

Optional: a squeeze of lemon or lime, fresh cilantro for garnish

🍳 How to Make It: 1️⃣ Sauté the base: Heat oil or butter in a large pot over medium heat. Add onion, garlic, and ginger. Cook for about 3–4 minutes until soft and fragrant. 2️⃣ Add spices: Stir in curry powder (or spice mix) and chili flakes if using. Let it sizzle for 30 seconds to wake up the flavors. 3️⃣ Add veggies & lentils: Stir in diced tomatoes, zucchini or bell pepper, and lentils. Mix well to coat everything in those spices. 4️⃣ Simmer: Pour in the broth or water. Bring to a gentle boil, then reduce heat to low. Cover and simmer until lentils are soft: ~20 mins for red lentils

~30 mins for green/brown lentils Stir occasionally and add more liquid if it thickens too much.

5️⃣ Creamy finish: Stir in the coconut milk (or yogurt/cream). Simmer for another 5 minutes until rich and silky. Season with soy sauce (or salt) to taste. 6️⃣ Brighten & serve: Remove from heat. Add a squeeze of lemon or lime for brightness. Garnish with fresh cilantro if you have it.

🍚 Serve with: Steamed rice

Warm naan or flatbread

A dollop of plain yogurt or raita on the side

🔑 Tips: ✅ If you like it thicker, mash a few spoonfuls of the lentils with your spoon. ✅ Want extra veggies? Spinach or kale stirred in at the end is chef’s kiss. ✅ Make it ahead — it gets even tastier the next day!

🥧 Rhubarb Kohupiima Sheet Cake with Cinnamon, Cardamom & Lemon Zest 🍰 Ingredients: Base: 1 pack Juubeli tordipulber (Jubilee cake mix, ~400–500 g)

~100 g melted butter

Creamy filling: 200–300 g kohupiim (quark)

1 egg

100 ml hapukoor (sour cream) OR milk/plant milk

50–70 g sugar (adjust to taste)

1 tsp vanilla sugar or ½ tsp vanilla extract

½–1 tsp ground cinnamon

¼ tsp ground cardamom

Zest of ½ lemon

Topping: 200–250 g fresh rhubarb, chopped

1–2 tbsp sugar (to sprinkle)

Optional: slivered almonds for extra crunch

🍴 How to make it: 1️⃣ Preheat oven to 180 °C (350 °F). Line a baking tray (approx. 20×30 cm) with parchment paper or lightly grease it. 2️⃣ Spread dry cake mix: Pour half to two-thirds of the Juubeli tordipulber evenly into the tray. Gently spread and press it flat — no mixing needed yet! 3️⃣ Add butter: Drizzle the melted butter evenly over the dry mix. This will create a soft base while baking. 4️⃣ Make the spiced creamy layer: In a bowl, whisk together kohupiim, egg, hapukoor (or milk), sugar, vanilla sugar, cinnamon, cardamom, and lemon zest until smooth. 5️⃣ Pour & top: Gently pour this spiced quark mixture over the dry base. Scatter chopped rhubarb on top. Sprinkle a little sugar over the rhubarb and scatter almond flakes if using. 6️⃣ Optional crumb finish: Sprinkle the remaining dry cake mix on top for a crumbly texture. 7️⃣ Bake: Bake for 35–40 minutes until the top is lightly golden and the creamy filling is set. 8️⃣ Cool & enjoy: Let cool slightly, slice into generous squares, and enjoy with coffee, tea, or a drizzle of extra cream.

✨ Taste: Soft, creamy, warmly spiced with cozy cardamom and cinnamon, brightened by fresh lemon zest — with tangy rhubarb and buttery cake crumb. Pure Baltic comfort! 🇪🇪💛

🫓✨ Easy Soft Naan (No Tandoor Needed) 🧂 Ingredients (makes 6 small naan): 2 cups all-purpose flour

2 tsp sugar

1 tsp salt

½ tsp baking powder

¼ tsp baking soda

½ cup plain yogurt or hapukoor

¼ cup warm water (adjust as needed)

1 tbsp oil or melted butter

Optional toppings: Melted butter with garlic (for garlic naan!)

Chopped parsley or cilantro

Sesame or nigella seeds

🍳 Instructions: 1️⃣ Mix the dough: In a bowl, combine flour, sugar, salt, baking powder, and soda. Stir in yogurt and oil. Add water little by little until you get a soft, slightly sticky dough. Knead briefly until smooth (3–5 mins). Add more flour only if too wet. 2️⃣ Rest: Cover and let rest at room temp for 30–60 minutes. This helps gluten relax and makes it super soft. 3️⃣ Shape: Divide dough into 6 pieces. Roll each one into a tear-drop or oval shape (~0.5 cm thick). Don’t worry if they’re rustic — that’s part of the charm! 4️⃣ Cook: Heat a dry skillet (cast iron if you have it) over medium-high. Place one naan in the pan — cook until bubbles form (~1–2 mins), flip, cook another minute until both sides are golden and slightly charred. 5️⃣ Brush & serve: Brush with melted butter (garlic butter if you dare), sprinkle herbs if desired — serve warm!

🌱 Tips: If using plant-based yogurt, just make sure it’s unsweetened and thick

For extra pillowy naan, you can add 1 tsp instant yeast and let the dough rise for 1–2 hours

Leftovers? Reheat on a pan or in foil in the oven — or use as wraps!

Yesss! 🙌 Now you’re leveling up to yeasted kefir naan — ultra soft, fluffy, and full of flavor. The kefir keeps it tender and slightly tangy, while the yeast gives you those pillowy bubbles and classic naan chew. 🫓✨

🫓💛 Yeasted Kefir Naan (Ultra Soft & Fluffy) 🧂 Ingredients (makes 6–8 naan): 2½ cups all-purpose flour

1 tsp instant dry yeast

1 tsp sugar

1 tsp salt

½ cup kefir (room temp)

¼–⅓ cup warm water

2 tbsp oil or melted butter

For brushing: Melted butter or garlic butter

Optional: chopped parsley, nigella seeds, sesame, flaky salt

🍳 Instructions: 1️⃣ Activate yeast (if using dry active yeast): If using active dry yeast, mix it with warm water and sugar, and let it bloom for 10 minutes. If using instant yeast, just mix it straight into the flour. 2️⃣ Make the dough: In a large bowl, mix flour, salt, and yeast. Add kefir, warm water, and oil. Mix until you get a soft, slightly sticky dough. 3️⃣ Knead: Knead for 5–7 minutes until smooth and elastic. Add a bit of flour if it sticks, but keep it soft — that’s the secret to fluffy naan! 4️⃣ Let it rise: Cover and let rise in a warm spot for 1–1½ hours, until doubled in size. 5️⃣ Shape the naan: Punch down the dough. Divide into 6–8 balls. Roll each one into a tear-drop shape or oval, about ½ cm thick. 6️⃣ Cook in a hot skillet: Preheat a dry cast-iron or nonstick pan until hot. Cook naan one at a time — about 1–2 minutes per side — until bubbles form and you see golden spots. 7️⃣ Brush & serve: Brush each hot naan with melted butter (or garlic butter 😍). Sprinkle herbs or seeds if you like.

🧄✨ Optional flavor boosts: Add 1 tsp garlic powder or onion powder into the dough for extra depth

Mix grated cheese into half the dough for cheesy naan pockets

Or even stuff with mashed spiced potato for a naan-meets-paratha moment!
